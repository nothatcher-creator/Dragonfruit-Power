# Vehicle Forge

A Fabric 1.20.1 mod (Loader 0.16.9+, Fabric API 0.92.2+) that adds 21 drivable vehicles, all with modeled interiors, working gauges, engine sounds and particles.

## Vehicles

| Group | Vehicles |
|---|---|
| Road | Classic Cruiser, Sedan, Sports Car, Pickup Truck, Van, Semi Truck, Fire Truck (siren + water cannon) |
| Bikes | Bicycle, Motorbike (2 seats), Dirt Bike |
| Military | Army Jeep (roof machine gun), Tank (aimed cannon), Rocket Artillery (8-rocket salvo) |
| Air & space | Helicopter (rockets), Propeller Plane (guns), Jet (rockets, afterburner), Rocket Ship |
| Rail & water | Steam Train (add up to 6 carriages), Speedboat, Yacht |
| Living | RV with a walk-in camper room you can build in and sleep in |

## Controls

- **W/S** drive or throttle, **A/D** steer, **Space** handbrake or climb.
- **B**: horn, fire weapon, or toggle the water cannon.
- **N**: siren or horn.
- **M**: fly down (helicopter).
- Turrets, cannons and the fire hose aim where you look.
- Planes take off once they reach speed. Look up to climb.
- Rockets: hold Space to launch.
- Trains: place on rails, and right-click with a Train Carriage to add carriages.
- RV: sneak + right-click from outside to enter your room. Click the door inside to leave.
- Right-click a vehicle with dye to paint it.
- Hit a vehicle to damage it. When it breaks, it drops as an item.

On phone launchers, map B, N and M to touch buttons.

## Building

This lives on the `vehicle-forge` branch of the Dragonfruit-Power repo. Each push builds the jar, and GitHub publishes it as a Release named "Vehicle Forge build N".
